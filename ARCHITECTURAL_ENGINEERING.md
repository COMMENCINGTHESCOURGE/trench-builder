CRITICAL_ANALYSIS = """
1. DATA INTEROPERABILITY CRISIS
   ─────────────────────────────
   The biggest obstacle to integrated architectural engineering is NOT
   computational power — it's data format wars.
   
   BIM files (Revit .rvt, IFC) don't talk natively to:
   - CFD simulators (OpenFOAM, ANSYS)
   - Structural FEA (SAP2000, ETABS)
   - Energy models (EnergyPlus, IES-VE)
   - Rendering engines (Unreal, V-Ray, Three.js)
   
   Each handoff loses fidelity. "Round-trip" workflows are marketing fiction.
   The industry needs: universal geometry kernel + physics metadata standard.
   
   TRENCH BUILDER advantage: Single-file HTML avoids format wars entirely.
   Everything lives in the same context — geometry, materials, physics, rendering.

2. LIABILITY IN AI-GENERATED STRUCTURES
   ──────────────────────────────────────
   If an AI topology optimizer suggests a beam layout and it fails:
   - Who is liable? The engineer who approved it? The software vendor? The AI?
   - Current legal frameworks assume human professional judgment.
   - Black-box neural networks cannot explain their reasoning in court.
   - "The algorithm suggested it" is not a legal defense.
   
   Solution trajectory: Explainable AI (XAI) + human-in-the-loop certification.
   The engineer must remain the legally responsible party.

3. THE DIGITAL DIVIDE IN CONSTRUCTION
   ───────────────────────────────────
   Advanced parametric tools require:
   - Expensive software licenses (Rhino $995 + Grasshopper + plugins)
   - High-end hardware (GPU workstations)
   - Specialized training (years of experience)
   
   Small firms and developing economies are locked out.
   The gap between 'starchitect' firms and everyone else is widening.
   
   TRENCH BUILDER counter: Single-file HTML runs on any device with a browser.
   Zero licensing cost. Democratizes access to 3D construction visualization.

4. OVER-AUTOMATION RISK
   ──────────────────────
   Fully automated construction eliminates:
   - Craft knowledge (carpenters, masons, metalworkers)
   - Site adaptation (humans catch what robots miss)
   - Cultural building traditions
   
   The goal should be AUGMENTATION, not replacement.
   Robots handle repetitive precision; humans handle judgment, adaptation, craft.

5. EMBODIED CARBON BLIND SPOTS
   ─────────────────────────────
   Most digital tools optimize for operational carbon (energy use) while
   ignoring embodied carbon (manufacturing, transport, construction waste).
   
   Steel and concrete dominate because they're computationally predictable,
   not because they're environmentally optimal.
   
   Needed: Real-time embodied carbon feedback in every design tool.
   TRENCH BUILDER opportunity: Add carbon cost overlay to material selection.
"""

  Disassembly sequencing — end-of-life recovery without destruction
