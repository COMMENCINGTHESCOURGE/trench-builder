01
Brutal Fantasy

The Remorseful Warlord

A fallen knight-king in his late 50s, haunted by past atrocities. He has renounced his throne and wanders, but his ornate, battle-scarred armour betrays his former status. The mood is heavy, introspective, and cold.

Height: 195 cm
Age: ~58
Build: Broad, stooped
Anatomy & Physique
  • Tall (195 cm), broad-shouldered but now slightly hollowed by age and guilt
  • Musculature still dense but softened, with a slight kyphotic stoop from decades wearing heavy plate
  • Heavy calloused hands with prominent knuckles, cracked nails, and a missing tip on the left ring finger
  • Thick wrists wrapped in dirty linen wraps that have unravelled slightly
Skin & Subsurface
  • Base: Pale, cool-undertoned skin with translucent quality. Deep reddish-orange SSS for thin areas (ear rims, nostril wings, eyelids), bluish tint for stubble shadow
  • Micro-texture: Heavy sagging pores on nose and cheeks. Deep forehead furrows, pronounced nasolabial folds. Clusters of broken capillaries across nose and upper cheeks
  • Imperfections: Long-healed vertical sword scar from brow to jaw on right side — waxy scar tissue with no pores, higher roughness, no subsurface. Liver spots on temples
  • Roughness: Oily shine on nose/forehead, matte cheeks, flaky dry skin around scar. Dirt ingrained into every wrinkle via baked cavity map mask
Face & Expression
  • Hooded grey-blue eyes with pronounced tear troughs. Lower lids slightly red and watery. Irides have faint arcus senilis and goldish flecks
  • Heavy, furrowed brows, always slightly knitted. Thick nose broken at least twice and set imperfectly
  • Thin, chapped, slightly down-turned lips. Lower lip bears vertical split scar. Yellowed teeth, one bicuspid replaced with crude iron replica
Hair & Grooming
  • Facial hair: Grizzled, uneven beard of coarse wiry grey and faded copper. Patchy density with folliculitis bumps on neck. Strand-based with multiple clump maps
  • Scalp: Thinning at crown, long greasy strands pulled back into failed warrior's knot. Dual specular lobe shader (sharp for grease, soft for dry fibers)
Clothing & Armor
  • Chestplate: Etched ceremonial Damascus steel, corrosion-obscured. Deep axe scratches exposing raw steel, black rust on corners. Faded phoenix design half obliterated by melt-burn
  • Fur mantle: Massive waterlogged wolf-pelt. Guard hairs clumped with rain and dried blood. Amber-to-dirt-brown gradient tips
  • Leather tunic: Cracked dark oxblood leather. Sinew cross-stitching, some stitches snapped and frayed
  • Trophy belt: Desiccated ears on twisted cord. Mummified waxy translucency with SSS mimicking old candle wax
Micro-Details
Rain droplets mid-bead on fur Droplet trembling at nose tip Frayed linen micro-fibers Iron tooth IOR edge rust Gold inlay catching warm light
Suggested Lighting

Overcast HDRI with soft, cyan-tinted ambient light. A single warm sodium-tone key light from a nearby censer brazier catching the edge of the cheekbone and the metal's gold inlay, contrasting with the cold fill.

02
Bio-Punk / Cosmic Horror

The Deep-Sea Archiviste

A humanoid creature from an abyssal civilization, this archivist breathes through gills and sees via bioluminescent organs. It floats within an ancient library carved from a leviathan's skull. The mood is eerie, silent, and sacred.

Height: 220 cm
Type: Androgynous
Build: Elongated, slender
Anatomy & Physique
  • Androgynous, slender, elongated (220 cm). Graceful but unnerving limbs — extra phalanges on long, triple-jointed fingers
  • Serene, permanent slouch as if suspended in fluid
  • Ribcage externally visible, coated in semi-transparent chitinous webbing that glows faintly
  • No navel — instead a spiral scar of bioluminescent nodes connecting torso to floating detached spinal column trailing behind like a vestigial tail
Skin & Subsurface
  • Base: Smooth rubbery mantle like deep-sea cephalopod. High SSS with very short mean free path (dense, milky). Ghostly translucent white with indigo/violet chromatophores beneath surface that slowly shift
  • Micro-texture: Pavement-like pattern of microscopic papillae (shark skin). Custom tileable normal map blending into fading fingerprints and dermal denticle texture
  • Bioluminescent markings: Freckles of symbiotic bacteria emitting steady teal (#00FFCC) glow. Edges bleed into skin via subsurface glow map. Brighter where skin is thinner (cheekbones, knuckles, cranial ridge)
Face & Expression
  • No nose, only a subtle vertical slit
  • Massive solid-black eyes with no sclera. Cornea faceted like compound eye, reflecting multiple tiny specular highlights. Wet nictitating membrane blinks sideways
  • Four soft prehensile maxillary palps (nautilus-like) covered in taste-sensitive cilia. Palps constantly undulate, wrapped over central bioluminescent vocal sac
Symbiotic Filaments
  • Long wafting sensory filaments from scalp and shoulder blades — like anglerfish barbels
  • Translucent hollow tubes filled with viscous golden ichor
  • Semi-rigid curves with finite thickness, capped with bioluminescent lure
  • Custom dielectric shader: high IOR (1.5), thin-film interference for pearlescent sheen, total internal reflection
Attire & Integrated Tech
  • Data mantle: Countless razor-thin hexagonal crystalline plates hovering 2 cm above body in magnetic field. Each plate etched with sub-micron glyphs. Anisotropic shader with directional roughness
  • Interface rods: Four chrome-smooth bone-like rods piercing floating ribs, glowing with data-pulse rings. Entry wounds healed with swollen calcified keloids. Rainbow titanium oxide passivation layer
  • Censer chain: Rust-free platinum chain across hips holding volcanic glass spheres containing dead star memories. Volume absorption shader for true depth
Micro-Details
Condensation on metal rods Parasitic barnacles on spine Nictitating membrane half-deployed Glyphs catching acute light Chromatophore shifting
Suggested Lighting

Deep abyss darkness (95% black). Three-point lighting using bioluminescent sources: primary from creature's own glowing nodes, rim light from distant giant siphonophore, subtle fill from crystalline plates' internal refraction. Volumetric fog with high anisotropy (0.8).

03
Optimistic Post-Cyberpunk

The Solar Punk Drifter

A 22-year-old courier and solar-turbine mechanic from a reclaimed desert metropolis. Her gear is a vibrant blend of repaired tech, living moss cultures, and hand-woven textiles. The mood is warm, dusty, and full of kinetic energy.

Height: 168 cm
Age: 22
Build: Lean, wiry
Anatomy & Physique
  • Lean, wiry build with toned arms and shoulders from climbing turbine towers daily
  • Distinct tan lines: sharp contrast between sun-darkened forearms/face and paler torso
  • Hyper-flexible joints (slightly double-jointed fingers) with prominent veins on back of hands
  • Short practical fingernails painted with protective copper-infused lacquer, chipped at edges
Skin & Subsurface
  • Base: Warm olive skin with high melanin concentration, golden-brown hue. Rich saturated red-orange SSS glow in direct light (ear rims, between fingers)
  • Micro-texture: Fine even pores across nose and inner cheeks. Ephelides (freckles) across nose bridge and upper shoulders, varying in size and density
  • Imperfections: Faint hyperpigmentation patch on left jawline from childhood burn. Iron-oxide sand caked into skin's micro-folds and hair follicles. Sweat trails carving rivulets through dust on temples
  • Ceremonial scars: Three delicate raised cicatrix lines on each bicep forming circuit-board pattern. Smoother, shinier scar tissue than surrounding skin
Face & Expression
  • Wide-set almond-shaped hazel eyes with permanent crow's feet from squinting into sun. Deep limbal ring, amber central heterochromia burst around pupils
  • Broad flat nose bridge. One eyebrow has tiny notch shaved through it, with a freckle exactly in the gap
  • Wide genuine smile creating deep dimples. Healthy teeth with custom golden filigree bicuspids forming small sunburst pattern when biting down
Hair & Grooming
  • Style: Thick tightly coiled 4B hair, shaved close on sides/back, erupting into high-top of free-form coils. Deliberately messy with threaded brass beads and solar-charging fiber-optics
  • Grooming: Each coil is clump of curves with high children count. Custom anisotropic shader for light catching tight spirals. Sun-bleached tips from deep black to coppery-brown
  • Peach fuzz: Downy translucent vellus hair on cheeks, jawline, upper lip catching golden rim light
Clothing & Gear
  • Turbine jacket: Cropped collarless jacket from decommissioned aerogel insulation fabric. Matte rubbery texture with prismatic sparkle. Patched with indigo Kente cloth and orange Sashiko stitching
  • Undershirt: Sweat-wicking recycled polymer mesh, pilled and frayed at hem. Translucency reveals darker skin beneath
  • Harem pants: Repurposed cargo-parachute silk dyed with oxidized copper to deep teal. Integrated 3D-printed gyroid knee-pad system with trapped micro-dust
  • Goggles: Diamond-like carbon coated lenses with magenta/cyan dichroic reflection. Cracked rubber seal repaired with waxed thread
  • Living moss: Bioluminescent symbiotic culture in glass capsule necklace. Two-sided translucency shader, roughness varying from velvety (dry) to glossy (wet)
Micro-Details
Gyroid pads deforming silk Fingerprint smudges on lenses Copper lacquer chipped in crescents Backlit hair split end Moss drip irrigation wet spots
Suggested Lighting

Golden hour sun (Key: rich amber/orange, low angle ~15°). Fill from giant bouncing white stucco wall providing soft warm bounce. Rim light is direct setting sun creating fire-rimmed silhouette around hair flyaways and peach fuzz. Faint volumetric spotlight on moss necklace.

Each character hinges on the interplay between macro silhouette and micro imperfection.
